Core Design

Seven Pillars of Avyra

Every feature is designed around one of these pillars. If it doesn't serve a pillar, it doesn't ship.

🌍
Explore
Hand-crafted 3D zones — city plazas, forest trails, rooftop lounges, underwater galleries.
🧑
Express
Deep avatar customisation. Wear, animate, and emote your unique identity.
💬
Connect
Spatial voice, text chat, gestures. Meet strangers or host private rooms.
🎮
Play
Accessible mini-games embedded in the world — no separate launcher, no friction.
💰
Earn
Complete quests, sell creations, win tournaments — all rewarded with WorldCoins.
🏢
Work
Branded VR meeting rooms with screen-share, whiteboard, and attendance controls.
🛠️
Create
Upload Blender models, script behaviours, and publish to the Creator Marketplace.

In-World Economy

Dual-Currency System

A transparent, inflation-resistant economy designed to reward real participation and fair creators.

🪙

WorldCoin (WC)

The free-to-earn in-game currency. Gained through quests, daily logins, events, and selling items. Never purchasable directly — keeps it fair.

+25 Daily Login +50–200 Quests +30–150 Mini-Games +75 Events 500 WC / day cap
💎

GoldCoin (GC)

Premium currency purchased with real money. €1 = 100 GC. Used for premium items and room upgrades. Convertible to WC at 1:10 — one direction only.

€1 = 100 GC GC → WC (1:10) Premium Items Room Upgrades Limited Editions

Technology

Built on the Open Web

No native app. No locked ecosystem. Avyra runs fully in the browser using open-web standards.

Layer Technology Why Chosen
3D / VR EngineBabylon.js 6Best WebXR support, built-in physics & PBR
VR APIWebXR Device APIBrowser-native, works on Quest 2/3 & PCVR
MultiplayerSocket.IOReal-time room sync with automatic fallback
Voice ChatWebRTC + Agora SDKLow-latency spatial positional audio
BackendNode.js + ExpressJavaScript everywhere, huge ecosystem
DatabaseFirebase FirestoreReal-time sync, built-in auth, generous free tier
Asset CDNCloudflare R2Zero egress fees, global edge network
3D AuthoringBlender 4+Open source, GLB export, large community
